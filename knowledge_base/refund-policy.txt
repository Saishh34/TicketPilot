Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 7 days of purchase.

Refund requests must include the order ID and registered email address.

Once a refund is approved, the amount is normally returned within 5 to 7 business days.

Digital products that have already been fully consumed may not be eligible for a refund.

If a payment was deducted but the order remains pending, the customer should wait up to 24 hours before contacting support.

If the order remains pending after 24 hours, the Payments Support Team should investigate the transaction.
